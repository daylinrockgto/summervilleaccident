## 2. Naming Rules

The four September 15 rules still hold. The H1 and the slug are the same string. Parents use Attorneys and children use Lawyers. Parents sit at the root and children nest under their parent. /practice-areas/ and /locations/ keep their slugs.

Three page types are new, and each follows the H1 equals slug rule.

**City pages** are county children. Each H1 names the city and uses Lawyers, for example Goose Creek Car Accident Lawyers under the Berkeley County parent.

**Answer pages** nest under their practice parent. The H1 is the question in the words people search, and the slug is that question lowercased and hyphenated. They are exempt from Attorneys and Lawyers, because they answer a question rather than name a service. The first paragraph answers the question directly in 40 to 60 words.

**The Spanish page** sits in a language folder, /es/, and its slug is its H1.

**Blog posts** sit at the root, never under /blog/. A post's slug is its full H1, lowercased and hyphenated, with punctuation dropped and every word kept. /blog/ stays as the blog index. Daylin set this on October 8, 2026.
