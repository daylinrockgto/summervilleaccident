Can a Rider Who Was Lane Splitting Still Recover?

Possibly, because South Carolina compares fault instead of barring every rider who broke a rule. The state Supreme Court adopted that approach in Nelson v. Concrete Supply Co., decided in 1991. Helmet, Goggle and Windscreen Rules for Riders Under 21
