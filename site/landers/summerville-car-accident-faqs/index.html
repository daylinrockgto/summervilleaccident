The Questions We Hear Most

Do I have a case?

Most strong claims have three things in common. You were hurt, someone else was at least partly at fault, and there is insurance to pay. If that sounds like your crash, call us or send the form. We will tell you honestly, for free.

How long do I have to file a car accident claim in South Carolina?

Usually three years from the crash to file a lawsuit.1 Claims against a city, county or state agency must be filed within two years.2 Evidence disappears much sooner, so call early.

What if I was partly at fault?

You can still recover if you were 50% or less at fault. Your amount is reduced by your share of the blame.3 Do not accept an adjuster's opinion on fault. That is a question the evidence answers.

What is my case worth?

No two cases are alike. The value depends on your injuries, your treatment, the income you lost, how fault is shared and how much insurance is available. No lawyer can promise an amount. After we review your crash we will tell you what we see, honestly.

I feel okay. Should I still see a doctor?

Yes. Adrenaline hides pain, and neck, back and head injuries often show up days later. A visit to the emergency room or urgent care protects your health, and it creates the record an insurer will ask for.

The insurance company already called me. What do I say?

Give them your name and your attorney's number. Do not give a recorded statement or accept a quick check before you talk to a lawyer. A release ends your claim for good.

What if the other driver had no insurance, or drove off?

Every South Carolina auto policy must include uninsured motorist coverage, so your own policy may pay.4 We review your policy for free and explain the options.

How do I get my crash report?

Summerville Police handle crashes in town and the Highway Patrol handles I-26 and the highways. We can obtain the report for you, and we read it with you so you understand what the officer wrote.

How long does a case take, and will I have to go to court?

It depends on your recovery. Nobody should settle before the doctors know the full picture. Many claims resolve without a lawsuit, and some need one. We prepare every case as if it will be tried, which is what gets the insurer to pay attention.

Yes. We represent people across Dorchester, Berkeley and Charleston counties, including Ladson, Goose Creek, North Charleston and Moncks Corner.
