What to Do After a {{NOUN_TITLE}} in {{PLACE}}

Short on time? Do these, then call us. We handle the rest.

  1. Call 911 and get checked out. Go to the emergency room or urgent care, even if you feel okay.
  2. Take photos. Cars, plates, the scene, your injuries. Get witness names.
  3. Get the crash report. The investigating agency files it with the state. We can obtain it for you.
  4. Don't give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurance company.
  5. Keep every bill and receipt, and track the days of work you miss.
  6. Talk to a lawyer before you sign anything. A release ends your claim.

What You May Recover

Every case is different. These are the damages South Carolina injury claims commonly include.

Medical bills

ER, surgery, therapy and the care you will need later.

Lost wages

Time missed from work, and lost earning ability if you can't go back.

Pain & suffering

Physical pain and the toll on your daily life.

Permanent injury & scarring

Lasting impairment or disfigurement.

Property damage

Repair or replacement of your vehicle, plus a rental.

Wrongful death

Damages for families who lost a loved one.
