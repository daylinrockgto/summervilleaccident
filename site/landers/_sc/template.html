South Carolina Law That Shapes Your Claim

Three years to file

Most injury lawsuits must be filed within three years of the crash.2 Claims against a city, county or state agency must be filed within two years.3 Evidence disappears much sooner, so call early.

Partly at fault? You can still recover

South Carolina uses modified comparative negligence. You can recover if you were 50% or less at fault, and your amount is reduced by your share of the blame. Once your share passes 50%, there is no recovery.4

Uninsured and underinsured drivers

Every South Carolina auto policy must include uninsured motorist coverage, so your own policy may pay when the other driver had none.5
