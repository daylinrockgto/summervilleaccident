What to Have Ready

Nothing is required. If you have any of these, they help us answer faster.

The crash report

Or the report number, or the name of the agency that responded. We can get the report for you.

Photos

The cars, the scene, your injuries. Anything on your phone.

Insurance information

The other driver's, and your own policy if you have it handy.

Where you were treated

Emergency room, urgent care, your doctor. Dates are enough.

Anything the insurer sent

Letters, a check, a release. Do not sign anything before we talk.

Your questions

Write them down. The call is for you.

Why Sooner Is Better

Camera footage gets overwritten, witnesses forget and insurers call early for a reason. A short call now protects evidence that cannot be recovered later, and it keeps you ahead of the filing deadlines. There is no cost to find out where you stand.
