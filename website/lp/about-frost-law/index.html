Meet Tara and Jack

Two attorneys, two very different backgrounds, one goal. When you call, one of them picks up.

Tara L. Frost

Attorney · Former Dorchester County Magistrate Judge

Tara leads the firm's injury practice. She served as a Dorchester County Magistrate Judge, the court that hears South Carolina's smaller civil claims, so she has watched insurance disputes from the bench. She focuses on car, truck and other injury claims. Charleston School of Law. Admitted to the South Carolina Bar in 2012.

Jack C. Frost

Attorney · 14 years in Lowcountry law enforcement

Jack spent fourteen years with the Summerville Police Department and the Charleston County Sheriff's Office, where he served as a detective. He has worked crash scenes, read the reports and interviewed the witnesses. Now he runs the firm's investigations. Summerville High School, College of Charleston, Charleston School of Law. Admitted in 2018.
