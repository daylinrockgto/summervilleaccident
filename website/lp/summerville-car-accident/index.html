You were hurt

What to Do After a Car Accident in Summerville

Short on time? Do these, then call us. We handle the rest.

  1. Call 911 and get checked out. Go to the emergency room or urgent care, even if you feel okay.
  2. Take photos. Cars, plates, the scene, your injuries. Get witness names.
  3. Get the crash report. Summerville Police in town, the Highway Patrol on I-26. We can obtain it for you.
  4. Don't give a recorded statement to the other driver's insurance company.
  5. Keep every bill and receipt, and track the days of work you miss.
  6. Talk to a lawyer before you sign anything. A release ends your claim.

Summerville Car Accident FAQs

How long do I have to file a car accident claim in South Carolina?

Usually three years from the crash to file a lawsuit.3 Claims against a city, county or state agency must be filed within two years.4 Evidence disappears much sooner, so call early.

What if I was partly at fault?

You can still recover if you were 50% or less at fault. Your amount is reduced by your share of the blame.2

What does it cost?

The case review is free. You never pay out of pocket. We only collect a fee if we recover compensation for you, so there's no risk in reaching out. If there is no recovery, you owe no fee and no case costs.

Do you have an office in Summerville?

Yes. 128 Linwood Lane, Summerville, SC 29483, a few blocks from downtown. We also meet by phone or video, and can come to you if you are hurt.

What if the other driver had no insurance?

Every South Carolina auto policy must include uninsured motorist coverage, so your own policy may pay.5 We review your policy for free and explain the options.

The insurance company already called me. What do I say?

Give them your name and your attorney's number. Do not give a recorded statement or accept a quick check before you talk to a lawyer. A release ends your claim for good.
