Injuries From Car Accidents

These are the injuries we see most after crashes on I-26, Dorchester Road and the streets of Summerville. If yours is not listed, call anyway.

Neck and back injuries

Whiplash, herniated discs and strains that keep you out of work and out of sleep.

Concussions and brain injuries

Headaches, memory and mood changes after a blow to the head, even without a loss of consciousness.

Broken bones

Arms, wrists, ribs, hips and legs, including fractures that need surgery and hardware.

Spinal cord injuries

Partial or complete, with the lifetime of care that follows.

Shoulder, knee and joint injuries

Torn rotator cuffs, torn ligaments and injuries that need months of therapy.

Burns, cuts and scarring

Lasting marks and disfigurement, and the surgeries to treat them.

Internal injuries

Organ damage and internal bleeding that an emergency room finds, or should have found.

Injuries to children

Claims for a child are handled differently, and we walk parents through every step.

Wrongful death

When a crash takes a life, we help the family with the claim so they can focus on each other.

What Your Claim Can Include

Every case is different. These are the damages South Carolina injury claims commonly include.

Medical bills

ER, surgery, therapy and the care you will need later.

Lost wages

Time missed from work, and lost earning ability if you cannot go back.

Pain & suffering

Physical pain and the toll on your daily life.

Permanent injury & scarring

Lasting impairment or disfigurement.

Property damage

Repair or replacement of your vehicle, plus a rental.

Wrongful death

Damages for families who lost a loved one.
